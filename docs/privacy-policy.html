PocketCane Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

PocketCane does not share camera images, LiDAR data or other app data unless you choose to use an AI description feature. Camera preview, distance measurement and haptic feedback work on your device without sending data to us or to AI services.

PocketCane is developed by Gabriel C. This policy explains the optional AI features and your choices.

Camera and LiDAR

Live camera and LiDAR data are processed on your device for navigation feedback. The app does not upload or save a recording of this activity. LiDAR depth data is not included in AI description requests.

Optional AI Descriptions

After you allow AI photo sharing, tapping “Key Item” or “Environment” captures one photo and sends a resized JPEG and the app’s description instructions to third-party services. The services return text for the app to display and read aloud. Photos may include personal information visible in the scene, such as people, documents or addresses.

  • Photos are sent only when you request a description. Allowing photo sharing does not enable background or continuous uploads.
  • The recipients are an AI request-routing service and an AI model hosting service. A request may pass through more than one company to produce the description.
  • The services also receive network and request information, such as the connecting IP address, request time, selected model and token usage, to operate, secure and bill for the service.
  • We do not operate a server that stores your photos or descriptions. The app does not save AI photos or descriptions as a history or to your photo library.

AI Service Providers

The AI provider list identifies the services used by the current app and links to their privacy information. The app identifies the recipients before requesting your permission. Models and providers may change as the service develops; the provider list is maintained separately so this policy does not depend on a particular model.

We do not send each photo to every provider we have evaluated. Requests go only to the recipients configured for that app version. Changes to recipients or material changes to processing require updated disclosures and renewed permission before further AI sharing.

Providers may process requests outside your country. Their processing, security, retention and any legally required records are governed by their applicable service terms. Provider-specific information is available in the provider list. The app requests routing that excludes providers permitted to train on submitted content; this is not a promise that all services retain no operational records.

Your Choices

You can decline AI photo sharing and continue using camera, LiDAR and haptic feedback. You can read this policy before allowing AI photo sharing. To read the policy, open “App Information” and choose “Privacy Policy”. To change AI sharing permission, choose the “Privacy” VoiceOver action on “Key Item” or “Environment”, or touch and hold either button and choose “Privacy”. Turn off “AI photo sharing” there to revoke permission for future requests. A request already sent cannot be recalled.

Contact us below with questions or requests about data processing. Because the app has no user account or photo history, we may need the approximate request time to help investigate a request with a service provider. Applicable law may give you rights to access, correct or delete personal data, restrict its use, or complain to a data protection authority.

Local Storage and Other Collection

The app stores onboarding completion and your AI sharing permission on your device. It does not include advertising or analytics SDKs, require an account, access GPS location, or send app usage analytics. Third-party operational information associated with an AI request is described above.

The policy and provider list are bundled in the app and can be read offline. If you choose an external website link, your browser connects to that site under its own privacy practices.

Children’s Privacy

PocketCane is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly solicit their personal information. Contact us if you believe a child’s personal information has been submitted through an AI request.

Changes to This Policy

Updated versions are posted here with a revised date. We update this policy when our data practices materially change and request permission again where required.
